The pitch in one sentence

TrustLayer is AI that protects the whole life of a PayPal payment: it reads the conversation before you pay, has PayPal hold the money while it decides, and settles problems before they become disputes.

The pitch in 30 seconds (memorise this)

Card fraud tools look at the card. But the things that break trust in PayPal, like purchase scams, disputes, surprise holds and subscription traps, happen in the conversation around a payment, and the money usually moves before anyone checks.

Our idea: authorize first, let AI decide with evidence, then move the money. A PayPal authorization holds the buyer's money without charging it, and voiding it costs nothing. That hold gives AI time to look, to ask someone the buyer trusts, or to settle a problem fairly.

The AI must quote the exact chat lines behind every warning, and code checks each quote. The AI advises; only code moves money. On 80 held-out chats it paused all 40 scams and wrongly paused 3 honest buyers.

Before you press record

About 15 minutes of setup. Doing it in this order means nothing is slow or surprising on camera.

1. Wake the site and reset it

  1. Open trustlayer-e5mz.onrender.com. The free server sleeps; the first load can take about 50 seconds. Wait until the Overview page appears.
  2. Click the Audit log tab, then Reset demo, then OK. This clears old orders and starts a fresh audit log.

2. Start a TrustLayer Plus trial (off camera)

  1. Click Subscriptions. On the TrustLayer Plus card, click the yellow PayPal Subscribe button.
  2. Log in with the sandbox buyer account (SANDBOX_BUYER_EMAIL and password from your .env) and approve.
  3. A "TrustLayer Plus · Free trial" card now sits under Your subscriptions. Leave it there for scene B5.
  4. Bonus: PayPal now remembers your login, so the checkout popups on camera are fast.

3. Browser

  1. Chrome, one window, maximised, zoom 100% (Ctrl + 0). 110% is fine if text looks small in the recording.
  2. Hide the bookmarks bar (Ctrl + Shift + B) and close other tabs.
  3. Allow pop-ups for the site (the PayPal window is a popup).
  4. Turn on Windows Do not disturb so no notifications appear.

4. Recorder

  1. OBS Studio, or Xbox Game Bar (Win + Alt + R starts and stops). 1080p, 30 fps.
  2. Do a 10-second mic test and listen back. Quiet room, phone on silent.
  3. Finally: click the Overview tab and make sure you are at the very top of the page.

Pace and editing

Speak slowly, about 150 words a minute; the SAY text is sized for that. It is fine to stop talking while PayPal or the AI is loading: cut that dead time afterwards in Clipchamp (built into Windows). If you stumble, pause two seconds and repeat the sentence; trim the mistake later. Keep the cursor still while you talk about something, and move it slowly when pointing.

If something goes wrong on camera

What happensWhat to do or say
The site is slow on the first loadYou woke it too late. Stop, wait until it loads, start again. Always open it a few minutes before recording.
The PayPal window is slow or doesn't openKeep talking about the hold; cut the wait in editing. If it never opens, use "PayPal button not loading? Use redirect checkout".
The model line says "LLM unavailable: rules + local model"Groq is busy and the app fell back. Say: "The hosted AI is busy right now, so our own model and rules took over. That fallback is a feature." Then carry on.
The offer amount isn't $18Normal: the assistant decides within policy. Read the amount on screen.
The resolution assistant asks a question instead of offeringType "Yes, a partial refund is fine." and press send.
You clicked the wrong thingPause, go back, redo the step. Trim it in editing. Audit log → Reset demo if you need a clean start (then redo the Plus trial).
